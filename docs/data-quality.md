### The two COVID seasons, verified from the data

* **2019-20** has 1,059 games (a full 30-team season is 1,230). Games per team range 64 to 75 (distribution `{64: 1, 65: 4, 66: 2, 67: 1, 71: 2, 72: 10, 73: 8, 74: 1, 75: 1}`). The league suspended play on 2020-03-11: up to that date teams had 63-67 games. Play resumed 2020-07-30 in the bubble with **22 teams** playing 88 more regular-season games (8 games each); the other 8 teams' seasons ended at the suspension. Last game in the data: 2020-08-14. So 2019-20 is a legitimately uneven, shortened season, not missing data: **nothing downstream may assume 82 games**; availability must be computed per team from `team_games`.

## 11. Offseason data (Summer League and preseason), added 2026-09-24

Ingested by `src/ingest/nba_offseason.py`; per-event drop counts are in `nba_offseason_report.json`. Findings that shaped the design
(ADR 0012 D2 and D3), each pinned by a test:

* `playergamelogs` returns placeholder player ids and blank names for most pre-2024 Summer Leagues (2015 and 2016: zero overlap with
  `leaguegamelog`), so `leaguegamelog` is the canonical row source and `playergamelogs` only refines integer minutes where a row
  matches exactly.
* The 2019-20 "preseason" pull contains 822 rows of July 2020 bubble games; a calendar window drops them.
* The July 2026 Summer League has official points above the made-shot points on every team in every game (+7.8 per team-game);
  roughly 40% of free throws are absent from `ftm`/`fta`. Player sums equal team totals, so points are trusted and the rows are kept;
  the points identity is one-sided (`pts >= 2*fgm + fg3m + ftm`) and the excess is reported. Every other event obeys the exact identity.
* No Summer League was held in 2020. Preseason exhibitions against non-NBA clubs have player rows but no opponent team row (dropped).
* 50-70% of Summer League rows and roughly 90% or more of preseason rows belong to players in the `players` table (the rest never
  played an NBA game); the 2026 draft class was missing from `players` until `nba_incoming` added it.

## 12. Player profiles, draft combine, ESPN status (added 2026-09-24, ADR 0016)

* `player_profiles` (5,231 rows) comes from the `playerindex` payload already cached for the roster update. Its COLLEGE field holds the previous
  *organisation* (a college, a club such as "Sydney Kings", or a prep school), not necessarily a college; `prev_org_type` is a documented heuristic
  (an organisation listed by at least three USA-born players is a college). COUNTRY is null for 0.2% of rows. One `PERSON_ID` appears twice in the raw index (kept: first).
* The index only knows people who were on an NBA roster; players cut in camp who never played have **no row**, so index presence identifies future NBA players and is never used as a feature.
  It also lists `FROM_YEAR` as the first season on a roster, which for an injured first-round pick (Sorber) precedes his first game, so it is used only to exclude earlier pros.
* `draft_combine`: 847 rows, draft years 2015..2026; about 57% of rookies have a row (international stashes mostly do not).
* `espn_status_snapshots`: appended daily; on 2026-09-24 600 players (479 ACTIVE, 56 DAY_TO_DAY, 12 OUT, 53 no status); 580 mapped to NBA ids. ESPN's `lastNewsDate` is the only staleness signal.
* NBA official injury-report PDFs: no file for any preseason date probed (2025-10-03/08/13/16, 2024-10-04/16); present from 2025-10-20 and 2024-10-22.

## Wikipedia contract events (`player_contracts`, ADR 0019): parsing quirks

Written by hand, not by `src.ingest.quality`. Source: the cached Wikipedia team-season wikitext (CC BY-SA 4.0; only derived facts are stored). Quirks found while parsing 360 pages:

* Table shapes differ by era: `||` inline rows (2015-16), a "Signed" column that holds contract text (2016+), Date / Player / Terms tables with `rowspan` dates and shared "Two-way contract" cells (2019+), and `{{sortname|First|Last}}` player cells with no wikilink (38 Additions/Subtractions rows; `wiki_transactions`' first-link rule misses them, ADR 0019 Consequences).
* Contract length is stated in the table for 36% of contract events (early pages 80%+, 2022 onward 10 to 20%); newer pages give the date and player only. About 30 lengths exist only in a cited article's title (`terms_source = title`).
* Dates: a cell date (including `{{dts}}`) or a citation `date=` exists for 84% of rows (citation dates are upper bounds on the event date, ADR 0019 "Date provenance"); yearless dates ("July 6") are read in the page's start year for Jun-Dec, the next year for Jan-May; 35 written rows only have an `access-date`; a citation or access date counts only from 1 June of the page's start year. The yearless-date roll (ADR 0019) moves 0 of the 863 real yearless rows after a cross-check against the row's own citation.
* Empty or commented-out tables leave `|}-->` debris rows; plain-text player names, a linked "Name (basketball)" page, `Two 10-day contracts / 2-year contract` cells, `expired` short deals on departure rows and sub-header rows ("In-Season Additions") all needed explicit handling.
* Player match: 91.5% of 3,032 distinct (name, season) names (0 ambiguous); the rest are mostly G League, college and overseas names. Unmatched rows are dropped, never guessed.
* `player_contracts.parquet` is not regenerated by any scheduled job: an offline re-parse of the cached pages only changes when `wiki_contracts.py` changes (or pages are re-fetched, which is live Wikipedia traffic), so re-run `python -m src.ingest.wiki_contracts --seasons 2015-16:2026-27 --offline` by hand after editing the parser (ADR 0019).
* The COVID-19 offseason (Nov-Dec 2020) falls after the 1 Oct tag boundary, so those signings are invisible to the 2020-21 target (conservative for the leak guard).
